## Required final acceptance

The final PR head must pass the repository-required `cargo fmt` and `pre-merge CI` checks. Complete native and WASM SQL reports must pass their full output, allocation, and bidirectional timing gates. The [isolated macOS run](https://github.com/cognica-io/uqa-engine/actions/runs/34783983872) must independently pass all 102 workloads under the same 1.44 timing ceiling; its verification revision differs from `bbef474a` only in workflow runner selection. The historical macOS foreground result remains a timing failure at 2.125. Archive checks, published baselines, and passing owner tests do not substitute for these final results.
