The operator uses the `appspand` AWS profile and its existing `seoul` SSH key. Instance identifiers, SSH addresses, private keys, controller evidence and generated reports remain outside source control. Before executing a candidate, collect exactly 32 independent A/A pairs from the reference executable, alternating the order of the two named roles. Retain every observation, including outliers. For each workload let $a_i$ and $b_i$ be its paired positive slope estimates and define $E_i = \max(a_i/b_i, b_i/a_i)$ and $n = \max_{1\leq i\leq32} E_i$. Compute the bound from these reference observations only and sign it before the candidate starts. Do not replace this bound using candidate samples or increase the reviewed regression limit.

The explicit statistical assumption is exchangeability of the 32 reference-pair absolute multiplicative errors and the four subsequent candidate/reference-pair errors after accounting for the true performance ratio. Host isolation and environmental records support that assumption but do not prove it; workload-dependent noise or drift can violate it. Under this assumption, an arithmetic median of four paired ratios can leave the multiplicative interval $[1/n,n]$ only if at least two of the four future absolute errors exceed the maximum of the 32 calibration errors. By exchangeable ranks, that event has probability at most $4\cdot3/(36\cdot35)=1/105$. Ties make the bound conservative. Thus the interval has marginal prediction coverage at least $104/105 > 0.99$ for each workload's complete four-pair median estimator. This is not simultaneous engine-wide coverage or posterior certainty about a permanent host property. Record this assumption, exact sample count and coverage with the signed evidence; expire it at the end of the controlled boot/session.

Apply the existing qualification verifier to $[r/n,rn]$, where $r$ is the candidate's median paired ratio. Accept only when the entire interval satisfies the unchanged manifest limit, report regression only when its lower endpoint exceeds that limit, and report inconclusive otherwise. Arithmetic overflow, incomplete samples, missing control evidence or a changed sampling protocol must fail explicitly. Generated observations and signatures belong in ignored output directories or CI artifacts; retain only compact provenance and conclusions in reviewed documentation.
