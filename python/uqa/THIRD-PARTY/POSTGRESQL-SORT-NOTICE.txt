PostgreSQL range sorting adaptation

UQA SQL contains a Rust adaptation of PostgreSQL 18 src/include/lib/sort_template.h for range normalization. It replaces pointer arithmetic and nonlocal errors with owned slices, fallible selected comparisons and both cancellation scopes, without changing equal-bound permutation.

Source: https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/include/lib/sort_template.h
